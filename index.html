Three steps, every time

01

Submit

Your form posts to /api/submit/{'{'}publicApiKey{'}'}. Emaily checks the domain, CAPTCHA, and rate limits before it accepts anything.

02

Route

The submission is queued, matched to a template and a sending service, and picked up for delivery.

03

Deliver

You get a delivery record with status and history. Failed sends can be retried in one click from your dashboard.

Built for the backend you already have

No SDK to install. Point any form or server at your project's public key and Emaily takes it from there.
