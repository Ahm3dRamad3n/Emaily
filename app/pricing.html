What happens at your monthly limit?

On any plan, submissions past your monthly email limit are marked QuotaExceeded instead of being sent. On paid plans that status is tracked and visible in your history so you know exactly what was skipped; on the Free plan, going over simply blocks further sends until the next cycle.
