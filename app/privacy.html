Privacy Policy

Last updated: the date this policy is published.

This is not legal advice. Emaily is a student project built as part of coursework in a College of Computers and Information, to practice building a real, production-style system — it is not operated by a registered company or business entity. This document is boilerplate written to be clear and honest about what the project actually does, and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before this project is used to handle real users' data in production.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Emaily project (referred to here as "Emaily," "this project," or "we") — a form-to-email routing service built and maintained by its developer as an educational project. There is no company behind it.

2. Information we collect

Account information. Your email address and a hashed (never plain-text) password when you register.

Connected service credentials. If you connect Gmail or Microsoft, we store the OAuth tokens needed to send on your behalf, encrypted at rest. If you connect SendGrid, Resend, or SMTP, we store that API key or password, also encrypted at rest. In every case, only send permission is requested — never the ability to read your mailbox.

Submission data. When a form submits through your project, the field values and the rendered email are stored as a history entry so you can review and retry it later — unless the template that handled it has "Save to history" turned off, in which case none of that submission's content is stored at all.

Usage data. Standard web analytics (see Cookies & Analytics below).

3. How your information is used

Your information is used only to operate the service you've configured: authenticating you, sending email through the service you connected, recording submission history so you can see what happened, and enforcing the limits of your plan. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising.

4. Third parties this project sends data through

Emaily's entire purpose is routing email through an account you connect, so by design, submission content passes through whichever of these you've chosen to use:

  • Gmail (Google) — if you connect a Gmail account.
  • Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365 — if you connect a Microsoft account.
  • SendGrid or Resend — if you connect one of these by API key.
  • Your own SMTP provider — if you connect one with an app password.
  • Telegram — only if you enable Telegram alerts on a project.
  • Google Sheets — only if you enable Sheets logging on a project.
  • An AI provider — only to generate the optional summary of a delivered email.

5. Cookies & analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how it's used — which pages are visited and roughly how. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to do this. You can opt out using your browser's cookie settings or a browser extension such as Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. No other tracking or advertising cookies are used.

6. Data retention

Account data and submission history are kept for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account soft-deletes your data and revokes all of your refresh tokens; submissions from templates with history-saving turned off were never stored in the first place, so there's nothing additional to delete there.

7. Your rights

You can review, export (via the API), or delete your own data at any time from your account settings. You can disconnect any connected service, which removes its stored credentials. If you'd like something handled that isn't available directly in the dashboard, reach out through the support page.

8. Security measures

Connected-service credentials are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Sessions use short-lived, purpose-scoped JWTs with rotating refresh tokens and immediate logout revocation. More detail is on the Features page.

9. Children's privacy

Emaily is not directed at children, and knowingly collecting information from children is not intended. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us through the support page and it will be removed.

10. Governing jurisdiction

Because this project is not operated by a registered business, no specific governing jurisdiction is designated for this policy.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the project develops. Meaningful changes will be reflected here with an updated "last updated" note at the top of the page.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent through the support page.
